Ledger

Double-entry bookkeeping: every transfer's postings sum up to zero, so all of them do. The database refuses an unbalanced transfer; the alert "Ledger unbalanced" watches the same sum.

Bank accounts

The bank's own accounts may go negative: the cash desk brings money in, clearing holds it on its way to other banks, settlement mirrors the bank's account at the clearing house.

Clearing house

Chaos at the clearing house, for demos: a forward that is late or lost misses the 10-second deadline, so the payment is rejected with AB05 and both banks learn it.
