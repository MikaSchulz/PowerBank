Three areas, as in a real bank

Each area shows what its role needs and talks to its own part of the API. There is no login yet (#36): pick one.

Online banking For a customer: one account with its IBAN and balance, money to any IBAN, every transfer in and out. Back office For bank employees: approvals, payments under investigation, opening accounts, cash at the counter, every transfer. Operations For admins: the ledger check, the bank's own accounts, the clearing house's chaos settings, Grafana and Temporal.
